# Idle Restaurant Tycoon 3D

A 3D Idle Restaurant Management Tycoon game built with Godot 4 and companion mobile architecture.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Dynamic Room Architecture**:
  - **Grand Cocktail & Espresso Bar**: Continuous wooden counter modules, high-top stools, espresso machine, cocktail blender, and artisan glassware along the back wall.
  - **Central Buffet Island Pavilion**: Connected credenzas with fresh produce crates, self-serve coffee, and lush boxwood floral partitions.
  - **VIP Window Banquettes**: Luxury lounge seating and potted palms along the sunlit bay windows.
  - **Waiter Service Station**: Staging hub near the kitchen pass stocked with clean dishware and menus.
  - **Reception Lobby**: Royal Crimson Carpet Runner with gold metallic borders, Hostess greeting stand, and comfortable guest lounge.
  - **Exterior Facade**: Striped French bistro awning and glowing pink/cyan neon entrance sign (`CHEF'S BISTRO ★ OPEN ★`).

- **Idle Progression & Economy**:
  - **Table Unlocking Progression**: Tables 1–3 open by default; Tables 4–8 unlockable with in-game revenue ($150 to $1,100) featuring bouncy 3D billboard unlock buttons.
  - **Visual Tiers**: Tables and cooking stations physically evolve across Tier 1 (Rustic), Tier 2 (Gourmet Linen & Wine), and Tier 3 (Royal VIP Candelabra).
  - **Unlockable Signature Menu**: Classic Bistro Burger ($45), Truffle Tagliatelle ($115), Margherita Pizza ($240), and Deluxe Dragon Roll Sushi ($480).
  - **Staff Roster**: Waiters Marco, Sophia (Speed Runner), and Lucas (VIP Concierge); Chefs Gordon, Pierre, and Antoine (Sous Chef).

- **Gameplay Depth & AI**:
  - **Customer Patience Meter**: Real-time overhead billboarding patience bars (green → amber → red) with speedy tip bonuses (+30%) and walkout penalties.
  - **Busboy Table Turnover**: Diners leave dirty plates upon finishing; idle waiters automatically wipe and reset tables for new patrons.
  - **VIP Michelin Food Critic**: Periodic visits by demanding critics; timely service awards 5-star reviews, reputation boosts, and 3x cash bounties!
  - **Audio & Ambience**: Seamless restaurant murmur and kitchen clinking ambience with audio SFX for coin payouts, service bells, upgrade fanfares, and walkouts.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Godot 4 (3D Restaurant Tycoon)
1. Open Godot 4.3+ and import the project located in the `restaurant_tycoon_3d/` folder.
2. Launch `scenes/main.tscn` to start playing.
